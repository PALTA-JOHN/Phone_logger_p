Drop a Serial log (.txt) here, use Open log…, or press Connect for live data from the device.
Files never leave your phone or computer: they are read by this page only.
  • Reads the E lines (engine, boost, duty, warnings), the C blocks (CAN statistics) and the S lines (loop, heap).
  • Time axis: the t: field of the firmware, or the Serial (12:16:01.123 ->), or estimated from the S lines.
  • A live session can be saved with Save log and opened here again later.
